What we do

One workshop for the whole build

The workshop

Everything under one roof in Industrial Area, Nairobi

Our main facility runs twelve service bays, a dedicated fabrication shop, a paint and powder-coat line and a diagnostics lane — so your vehicle is never trucked between subcontractors mid-build.

  • Genuine and OEM-equivalent parts, sourced and warehoused in-house
  • Fixed written quotes before work begins — no surprise line items
  • Photo updates at every build milestone, on WhatsApp or email
  • Six-month workshop warranty on labour and fitted parts
  • KEBS-compliant conversions with inspection paperwork handled for you
