WORKSHOP FOOTAGE / BUILD RECORD

Independent workshops have a specific role

Dealer service remains the right path for eligible warranty work and recalls. Custom Cruisers is built for owners who need out-of-warranty maintenance, a conversion the dealer won't touch, a second opinion, or a clear technical explanation before approving major work.

A rattle on corrugations is the beginning of the investigation. Suspension, bushings, body mounts, drivetrain and electrical systems can produce overlapping symptoms — the vehicle is assessed before a responsible estimate can exist.

What the six service systems cover

Conversions cover park-compliant safari and game-drive builds. Suspension, mechanical and electrical cover everything from routine service to full rebuilds. Fabrication covers the steel: bars, sliders, racks and one-off brackets. Fleet contracts put all of it on a fixed per-vehicle schedule.

INTAKE RULE / 01

Appointments are required. Assessment comes first because the recommendation is based on inspection rather than a guess. The workshop obtains written approval before major work begins.
